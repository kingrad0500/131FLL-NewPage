## How to answer

Reply to the numbered items in any form — inline text, a document, or a photo
of a printed race guide. Anything left `unknown` renders as a visible `[TBD]`
badge and keeps blocking the production build, which is the safe default.

**Nothing here will be invented.** Race logistics are safety information; a
guessed cutoff time or aid-station count could genuinely harm a runner.

### Race information

- **What time do the races start?** The half marathon and relay start at
  6:15 a.m.; the 10K and 5K start at 7:00 a.m. Every distance starts and
  finishes at Las Olas & A1A.
- **Where is the start and finish?** Las Olas Oceanside Park,
  3000 E Las Olas Blvd, Fort Lauderdale, FL 33316.
- **How long is the course open?** Three hours and thirty minutes. After that
  the roads reopen and runners continue on the sidewalks as pedestrians.
- **Can I walk?** Yes. Walkers finish by 10:00 a.m., start behind the runners,
  and there is no early start.
- **How old do I have to be?** Half marathon participants must be at least 14.
  Younger runners need a parent-signed minor waiver. `[TBD: 10K, 5K, relay]`
- **Are there aid stations?** Eleven along the course with water and sports
  drinks. Medical help is available at each one, with ambulance and EMS bike
  teams covering the route.
- **Can I wear headphones?** Yes, but keep the volume low or wear a single
  earbud — you need to hear course marshals and traffic.
- **What is not allowed on the course?** Baby joggers, skateboards, bicycles,
  and animals.
- **Is the course certified?** Yes, the course is USATF certified.
- **Is there food at the finish?** Yes, a finish-line food tent for
  participants. Spectators cannot enter it.
- **Is the race capped?** Yes, the half marathon and the 5K both have
  participant caps. `[TBD: cap numbers]`

### Registration

- **How do I register?** Through the registration page, which opens RunSignup.
- **Is there race-day registration?** If space remains, race-morning
  registration runs 5:00–5:50 a.m. `[flagged for correction]`
- **Can I get a refund?** There are no refunds whatsoever. All race expenses
  are paid in full to our vendors months before the race is run.
- **Can I give my entry to someone else?** No. Entries are non-transferable.
- **Can I change distances?** Up to two weeks before race day.
  `[TBD: how, now that timing has moved to Split Second Timing]`
- **When does registration close?** `[TBD]`

### Packet pickup

- **When and where?** Saturday 7 November 2026, 8:00 a.m.–6:00 p.m., Downtown
  Events Center, 416 NE 1st Street, Fort Lauderdale, FL 33301, alongside the
  health and fitness expo. Race-morning pickup is also available at the
  registration tent from 4:30 a.m. `[flagged for correction]` We strongly
  recommend collecting your packet on the Saturday.
- **Can someone else collect my packet?** Yes, with a photocopy of your photo
  ID and your confirmation email.
- **What is in the packet?** Your bib with its gear-check tag, the timing tag
  attached to the back of the bib, safety pins, and a post-race beer wristband
  for participants 21 and over.
- **How do I wear my bib?** On the front, outside all clothing, for the whole
  race. Do not alter, fold, or wrinkle it. Bibs are non-transferable.

### Results and awards

- **How is the race timed?** Split Second Timing handles timing. The tag on the
  back of your bib records both clock time and chip time.
- **What is the difference between clock time and chip time?** Clock time runs
  from the starting gun; chip time runs from the moment you cross the start
  mat. Chip time is your personal result.
- **Where do I find results?** On the Results & Photos page, which links to
  every distance for the current year and the archive back to 2021.
- **Do I get a medal?** Half marathon finishers receive a commemorative
  finisher medal. `[TBD: other distances]`
- **Are there awards?** Age-group awards are presented. `[TBD: categories and
  age bands]`
